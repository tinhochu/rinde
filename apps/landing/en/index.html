How it works

No account and nothing to download from an app store. Open Rinde in your browser and start.

  1. Search or build your list

    Type what you need: rice, eggs, toilet paper. Rinde lines up the same product across the three stores, even when each one names it differently.

  2. See your plan

    Rinde splits your list across the stores so you spend less, and shows what you would pay at each one.

  3. Send your receipt and earn points

    Snap a photo of the receipt or forward the electronic invoice the store emails you. Your prices help others shop better, and you earn points and badges.

Where the prices come from

Every price in Rinde says where it came from and how long ago we saw it.

The stores' own catalogs

We read the prices D1, Jumbo and Éxito publish online and refresh them every day.

Other shoppers' receipts

When someone confirms their receipt, those in-store prices show up marked as from a receipt for a few hours, with the branch where they were paid.

AI reads receipts and recognizes matching products. It never writes a price.

You can use Rinde without an account. If you create one, your points follow you to any device.

Install it on your phone

Rinde installs from the browser and sits on your home screen like any other app.

Android

Open Rinde in Chrome and tap Instalar on the card on the home screen.

iPhone

  1. Open Rinde in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
